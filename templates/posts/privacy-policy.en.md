# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

Welcome to **The Chatbot**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our services via WhatsApp and the web app at [the-chatbot.irwinarruda.com](https://the-chatbot.irwinarruda.com).

## 1. Information We Collect

When interacting with our service, we collect the following types of information:

- **Identity Data**: Your name and phone number, as provided by WhatsApp, and the email address of your Google Account.
- **Authentication Data**: Google access tokens (Access and Refresh Tokens) to authenticate you and access Google services on your behalf.
- **Communication Data**: The history of your messages sent to The Chatbot.
- **Usage Data**: IDs of Google Sheets spreadsheets you configure for expense tracking.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide the conversational service via WhatsApp and the web app.
- Authenticate your identity using Google Accounts.
- Manage your financial data in your own Google Sheets upon your request.
- Process your messages using Artificial Intelligence to provide relevant responses.

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with specific providers to facilitate our service:

- **Meta (WhatsApp)**: For sending and receiving messages.
- **Google**: For authentication and access to your spreadsheets. We securely store tokens to maintain this connection.
- **AI model providers**: To generate responses, we send the content of your conversation to an AI model provider. This includes the spreadsheet data needed to answer your request, such as transactions, categories and account balances. Our default provider is Z.ai. If you choose another provider in your settings, your conversations are processed by that provider instead. Please refer to the provider's data policy for more details.
- **OpenAI**: We send voice messages to OpenAI to transcribe them into text.

## 4. Data Retention and Security

Your data, including chat logs and authentication tokens, is securely stored in our database. We retain this information to preserve the context of your conversations and ensure the continued operation of services such as expense tracking.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data directly through the chatbot commands or by contacting us. You can also revoke The Chatbot's access to your Google Account at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 6. Google User Data

The use and transfer of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

- We access only the spreadsheet you link to The Chatbot, and only to read and record your financial data at your request.
- We do not use user data obtained through Google Workspace APIs, including the content of your Google Sheets, to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
- We send spreadsheet data to an AI model provider only to produce the answer or action you asked for.
- We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
